About this Simulator

Disclaimer: It is easy to feel lucky when you have infinite pulls; do not let this tool give you a false sense of confidence. Good or bad results obtained here have zero impact on your actual in-game luck and should not encourage impulsive pulling. Luck does not transfer, accumulate, or "balance out" over time!

This fan-made simulator is for entertainment purposes only and is not affiliated with the game developers. Rates and pools are subject to change and remain unconfirmed until banners officially debut.


General Informations

  • Gems and Tickets can be manually adjusted by clicking on the currency values at the top of the screen.
  • There are no paid gems and all various ticket types are represented by one ticket.
  • Template banners are generic configurations that represent common banner types (Spotlight, Seasonal, etc.). They serve as presets to illustrate typical banner structures and should not be confused with real in-game banners.
  • Banner list provided is not exhaustive and may not include all past or present banners (Tiered Scout, Select Scout, ...)
  • Scout results and scout points for a banner will reset automatically if you have not pulled on it for 60 consecutive days.
